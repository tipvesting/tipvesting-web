What this is

Tipping got weird. Quitting isn't the answer.

The prompt starts at 18% for a coffee you poured yourself. Everyone feels it. But the person holding the terminal isn't the one who put it there — so refusing to tip mostly punishes the wrong person. This is the third option.

Tip smarter, not less

Know what the percentage is doing, why it's climbing, and where it's genuinely earned. Then decide on purpose instead of under pressure.

Invest what generosity costs you

Match every tip with a matching dollar into the market. Same money, twice the awareness. The receipt stops being a one-way door.

Question the system, keep your values

Being frustrated with businesses that offload payroll onto customers isn't the same as being cheap with the person serving you.
